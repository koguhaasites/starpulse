# Account Deletion Request

If you signed into our mobile app using Google Sign-In and wish to delete your account and associated data, you can request deletion below.

---

## What data will be deleted?
Upon receiving your request, we will permanently delete:
- Your user profile details (Name, Email address).
- Your saved app preferences and configurations.
- Any app usage history tied to your account identifier.

---

## How to request deletion
Please send an email from the Google account you used to sign into the app to:
**[admin@koguhaa.com](mailto:admin@koguhaa.com)**

- **Subject Line:** Account Deletion Request - [Starpulse]
- **Timeframe:** We process all data deletion requests within **15 business days**. You will receive a confirmation email once your account data has been completely erased from our servers.
